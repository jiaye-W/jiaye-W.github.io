Hello. I am a second-year PhD candidate at the [Chair of Discrete Optimization](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/disopt/) within the [Institute of Mathematics](https://www.epfl.ch/schools/sb/research/math/) at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), supervised by [Friedrich Eisenbrand](https://people.epfl.ch/friedrich.eisenbrand?lang=en).

Before that, I received my M.S. in Applied Mathematics from EPFL in 2024 and my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-SZ) in 2021.

## Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of discrete mathematics and theoretical computer science. 
In particular, I study cutting-plane methods, convex relaxation hierarchies, and extended formulations. 
I am also interested in stable matchings and submodular maximization.
